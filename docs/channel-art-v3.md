# Channel series V3

Eight original images generated with the built-in image tool, then resized and compressed with Pillow. Shared prompt: photorealistic Nordic editorial photography, landscape 3:2, navy shadows, natural warm morning highlights, restrained saturation, clear central subject readable at 80px and 300px, robust square and 16:9 crops. No text overlays, watermarks, globe, robot, American gavel, weapons or sensational crime reconstruction. Staged illustrations, not actual news photography.

Reviewed together in docs/evidence/v3-channel-series.jpg at full cover, 80px and larger square scale. Motifs are distinct; the violence square crop partly crops the patrol car but retains the Swedish street identity. All channels use the same original identity in cards, episode/article thumbnails and player, with responsive crops only. Illustrative caption retained on article pages. Images have empty alt text where adjacent channel/title already supplies the accessible label; clickable controls have descriptive names.

Variants per channel: 960x640 WebP quality 79, 400x267 WebP quality 75, 640x640 WebP quality 79. Combined 24 variants approximately 1.1 MiB. Responsive srcset selects small thumbnails, width/height reserves layout space, lazy loading is used below the first view. No image text overlays in channel cards, so typography stays legible in both themes. Original generated PNGs remain in the session's generated_images directory, and optimized project assets are committed in assets/channels.
